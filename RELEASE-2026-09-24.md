## Changes

- SDK: optional MPP purchase-lineage continuation, plus canonical strict UTF-8
  decoding that was missing from the development branch.
- PayKit: package/CLI rename, PAYKIT_* configuration with QUICKPAY_* fallbacks,
  PayKit links, hosted card-checkout link creation, manifest/price validation,
  and recovery of existing sessions after new payments are disabled.
- Checkout: DIRECT/DELEGATE PayKit routes, explicit checkoutSessionPath,
  checkoutType routing, and active-origin documentation.
- Server candidate: typed fiat checkout and existing-order recovery. The
  legacy HMAC delimiter ambiguity is unchanged and remains a release blocker;
  no server package/tag may be published as part of this release.
- All four candidates pin pnpm 10.28.0 and have valid local workspace roots.
  PayKit's optional dependency accepts SDK 0.2.x and 0.3.x; the frozen lockfile
  remains registry-resolved at 0.2.1, without workspace links or policy bypass.
